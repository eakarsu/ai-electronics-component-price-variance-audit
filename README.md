# Electronics Component Price-Variance Audit

Recover component overcharges and unsupported price variances across suppliers, brokers, and contract manufacturers.

**Primary buyer:** Electronics manufacturers and contract manufacturers. **Evidence:** purchase agreements, approved vendor lists, component parts, quotes, purchase orders, receipts, invoices, commodity adjustments, NCNR terms, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Component master registry
- Approved supplier mapping
- Quote agreement library
- Purchase order ingestion
- Receipt invoice matching
- Contract price validation
- Commodity adder audit
- Broker premium review
- NCNR term validation
- MOQ price tier
- Currency conversion
- Variance calculation
- Supplier dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Part supplier analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4706>. API: `5706`.
